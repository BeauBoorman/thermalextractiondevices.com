### Identity notes

- CBLV was one of the two new propyl cannabinoids from Thai cannabis in Shoyama 1981 [^2] (the other being Δ7-cis-iso-THCV); it is the CBCV-series photoisomer by analogy to CBL/CBC, whose photochemical formation was established by Crombie [^3].
