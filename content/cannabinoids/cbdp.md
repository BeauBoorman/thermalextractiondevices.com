## Cannabis occurrence

Trace natural constituent [^1]; commercial material is synthesized. Occurrence values are batch- and report-attached. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBDPA is the presumed precursor from the heptyl branch [^1]; CBDP forms by decarboxylation on heating. Dedicated enzyme studies were not located.

## Reported biological activity

### Human clinical evidence
None identified for CBDP (as of 2026-08-28).

### Preclinical animal and in vitro evidence

{{include includes/preclinical-evidence-note.md}}

Early computational/in-vitro work (2026) reports CBDP acting as a negative allosteric modulator at CB1 in expression systems [^2a]; this is preliminary and does not establish effects in people.

### Industry claims
"CBDP does what CBD does but stronger" is unsupported marketing; no clinical data exist.

- Uncharacterized
