# Cannabigerovarinic Acid (CBGVA)

## Identity

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred name | Cannabigerovarinic acid (CBGVA) |
| IUPAC name | 2-[(2E)-3,7-dimethylocta-2,6-dienyl]-3-hydroxy-5-propylbenzoic acid (condensed; confirm against the PubChem record before machine use) |
| CAS number | 64924-07-8 |
| PubChem CID | 59444383 |
| InChIKey | FAVCTJGKHFHFHJ-GXDHUFHOSA-N [^1] |
| Molecular formula | C20H28O4 |
| Molecular mass | 332.44 g/mol (332.4) |
| Compound class | Cannabinoid acid; propyl homologue of CBGA (open-chain resorcinolic acid) |
| Stereochemistry | E/Z geranyl-side-chain geometry; the natural acid is the E isomer, with cannabinerovarinic acid the Z partner [^1] |
| Major synonyms | Cannabigerovarinic acid, CBGVA; decarboxylation product is CBGV |

### Identity notes

- CBGVA was co-isolated with the other propyl acids from Thai cannabis [^2]; CAS 64924-07-8 corroborated by Cayman CRM 29787 and Cerilliant C-226 [^1].
- CBGVA is the branch point for the entire propyl (C3) series, standing to THCV/CBDV/CBCV-series acids as CBGA stands to the C5 series [^3]; the propyl-series elaboration (cannabicyclovarin, Δ7-cis-iso-THCV) is recorded in the group's 1981 Thai-cannabis paper [^3a].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBGVA is the least-volatile member of the propyl branch in practice: it sits upstream of the synthase products and decarboxylates to CBGV on heating [^2][^5]. Compound-specific device kinetics are unresolved. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace acid in most chemotypes, higher in propyl-rich material; measured values are batch- and report-attached [^6]. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBGVA is biosynthesized from divarinolic acid and geranyl pyrophosphate and is the shared substrate for the propyl-branch synthases [^3][^7]; it decarboxylates to CBGV on heating [^2].
