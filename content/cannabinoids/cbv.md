### Identity notes

- CBV is to THCV what CBN is to Δ9-THC: the aromatized oxidation product. Some analytics literature uses "CBNV"; the cannabinodivarin PubChem record (C19H22O2) appears to carry an inconsistent naming — verify structure before machine use [^1].

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace constituent of aged propyl-cannabinoid-rich material; measured batches report CBV only at trace levels tied to product age. No batch-attached dataset is cited here [^3]. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBV forms by oxidation of THCV (by analogy to the THC→CBN pathway, with no dedicated kinetic study located); the acid form CBVA is expected to decarboxylate to CBV on heating.
