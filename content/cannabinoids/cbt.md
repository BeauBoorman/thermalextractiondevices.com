### Identity notes

- CBT is one of several C21H30O4 cannabinoid triols; older literature (ElSohly 1984 book chapter [^1]) describes cannabitetrol and related polyhydroxylated cannabinoids — do not collapse the distinct triols into one identity.
- Hydroxylated cannabinoids are both plant constituents and THC oxidation/metabolism companions; chromatographic resolution from CBD and other co-formula triols is required.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace constituent; reported in aged or oxidized material alongside THC oxidation chemistry [^3]. No batch-attached occurrence dataset is cited here. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBT is not a major biosynthetic end-product; polyhydroxylated cannabinoids were characterized as trace constituents [^1], and hydroxylation accompanies THC oxidation chemistry in aged material.
