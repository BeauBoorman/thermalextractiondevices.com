### Identity notes

- Cannabinerolic acid is the Z partner of CBGA (E) — same formula, distinct geometry, distinct identity [^1]. Do not collapse geometric isomers.

## Biosynthesis and processing

Cannabinerolic acid occurs as the Z-geometric partner of CBGA in the plant's prenyl-benzoic acid network [^1].
