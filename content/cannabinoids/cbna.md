# Cannabinolic Acid (CBNA)

| Stereochemistry | Fully aromatic C-ring; no stereocenters, mirroring CBN |

### Identity notes

- CBNA was isolated from aged cannabis and characterized alongside CBGAM in Shoyama 1970 [^2]; it forms by oxidation of THCA (or CBCA-type rearrangement in some pathways), not by direct biosynthesis.
- CAS 2808-39-1 was verified by PubChem name resolution (the CAS string resolves to CID 3081990) [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBNA decarboxylates to CBN during heating; thermal release of the CBN series from botanical matrix proceeds via decarboxylation followed by vapor-pressure-driven evaporation of the neutral [^2][^4]. Compound-specific kinetics are unresolved. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

CBNA is a trace constituent that increases in aged, oxidized material; measured values are batch- and report-attached. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBNA is not a primary biosynthetic product: it forms by oxidation of THCA in aging material and decarboxylates to CBN on heating [^2].
