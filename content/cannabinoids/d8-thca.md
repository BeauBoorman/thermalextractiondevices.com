### Identity notes

- Δ8-THCA is a distinct acid identity from THCA-A (different double-bond position); thca.md records CAS 5957-75-5 as the neutral Δ8-THC — the acid itself is unresolved here [^2].
- Analytics literature reports it as a trace acid; no dedicated decarboxylation kinetics were located; the acid-series kinetic studies cover the C5 acids [^3].
