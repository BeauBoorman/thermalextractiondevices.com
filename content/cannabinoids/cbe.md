| Major synonyms | Cannabielsoin, CBE, CBE-A; a metabolite of CBD in mammals |

### Identity notes

- CBE forms from CBD both biotically (plant tissue cultures [^2]; mammalian metabolism [^3]) — it is simultaneously a plant constituent and a CBD metabolite.
- Not to be confused with CBEA (cannabielsoinic acid) or the propyl homologue CBE-C3 [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBE is a pre-existing minor constituent in thermal extraction rather than a device-temperature product; its formation from CBD in analytics contexts is oxidative/metabolic, not setpoint-driven [^2][^3]. No compound-specific release or degradation data exist. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace constituent in plant material and a reported CBD metabolite in mammals [^2][^3]; no batch-attached occurrence dataset is cited here. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBE forms from CBD by oxidative cyclization — demonstrated in Cannabis suspension cultures (Hartsel 1983 [^2]) and identified as a mammalian CBD metabolite (Yamamoto 1991 [^3]). The acid CBEA follows the analogous relationship to CBDA.

## Reported biological activity
