### Identity notes

- CBDVA was one of the four Thai-cannabis propyl acids [^2]; CAS 31932-13-5 corroborated by Cayman CRM 30245 and Cerilliant C-152 [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBDVA decarboxylates to CBDV during heating; compound-specific device kinetics are unresolved [^2][^4]. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace-to-minor acid in propyl-rich chemotypes (some landrace and CBDV-type material); measured values are batch- and report-attached [^5]. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBDVA is biosynthesized from CBGVA by CBDA synthase accepting the propyl substrate [^6] and decarboxylates to CBDV on heating [^2].
