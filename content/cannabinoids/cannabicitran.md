### Identity notes

- The "cannabicitran" identity is an ether-bridged rearrangement product in the CBC family [^2]; the abbreviation overlap with cannabitriol ("CBT") is a known naming hazard — this record uses the citran structure (CID 101820920) [^1]; CBC-type cyclization/photochemistry context per Crombie [^3].
