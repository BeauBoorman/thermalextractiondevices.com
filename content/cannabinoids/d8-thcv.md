### Identity notes

- Δ8-THCV is the Δ8 positional isomer of THCV (which itself is the propyl homologue of Δ9-THC); it is reported as a process byproduct in Δ8/THCV manufacturing and requires chromatographic resolution from THCV and Δ9/Δ8-THC [^1].
- This record is identity-anchored; the compound lacks a dedicated peer-reviewed physical-property literature of its own in the sources available here.
