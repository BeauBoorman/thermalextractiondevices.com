### Identity notes

- CBLA was isolated and characterized by Shoyama et al. in 1972 [^1]; it forms photochemically from CBCA rather than by direct biosynthesis.
- CAS 40524-99-0 is carried by the Restek compound registry; PubChem did not resolve it this session — treat as vendor-corroborated, not PubChem-verified.

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBLA is a pre-existing trace acid that decarboxylates to CBL on heating; compound-specific device kinetics are unresolved [^1]. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace acid in UV-exposed material; measured values are batch- and report-attached. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBLA is not directly biosynthesized: CBCA photoisomerizes to CBLA on irradiation [^4], and CBLA decarboxylates to CBL on heating [^1].
