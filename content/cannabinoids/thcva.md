# Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabivarinic Acid (THCVA)

## Identity

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred name | Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabivarinic acid A (THCVA-A) |
| IUPAC name | (6aR,10aR)-1-hydroxy-6,6,9-trimethyl-3-propyl-6a,7,8,10a-tetrahydro-6H-benzo[c]chromene-2-carboxylic acid |
| CAS number | 39986-26-0 |
| PubChem CID | 59444416 |
| InChIKey | IQSYWEWTWDEVNO-ZIAGYGMSSA-N [^1] |
| Molecular formula | C20H26O4 |
| Molecular mass | 330.42 g/mol (330.4) |
| Compound class | Cannabinoid acid; propyl homologue of THCA-A |
| Stereochemistry | Two stereocenters, (6aR,10aR) trans; A/B acid regioisomers exist, natural material is the A form [^1] |
| Major synonyms | THCVA, Δ9-THCVA-A, tetrahydrocannabivarinic acid; decarboxylation product is THCV |

### Identity notes

- THCVA was one of the four propyl cannabinoid acids co-isolated from Thai "Meao Variant" cannabis [^2]; CAS 39986-26-0 is corroborated by Cayman CRM 21259 [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

THCVA decarboxylates to THCV during heating and the neutral evaporates; compound-specific conversion rates under device conditions are unresolved [^2][^4]. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace-to-minor acid in propyl-rich chemotypes; measured values are batch- and report-attached [^5]. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

THCVA is biosynthesized from CBGVA by THCA synthase (accepting the propyl substrate) [^6] and decarboxylates to THCV on heating [^2].
