### Identity notes

- CBCVA was co-isolated from Thai cannabis [^2]; CAS 1628112-69-5 corroborated by Cayman CRM 32718 [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBCVA decarboxylates to CBCV during heating; compound-specific device kinetics are unresolved [^2][^4]. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace acid in propyl-rich chemotypes; measured values are batch- and report-attached [^5]. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBCVA is biosynthesized from CBGVA by CBCA synthase accepting the propyl substrate [^6] and decarboxylates to CBCV on heating [^2].
