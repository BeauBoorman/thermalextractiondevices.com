### Identity notes

- The propyl-cannabinoid series, including the cannabicyclovarin (CBLV) companion identity, was elaborated in Shoyama's Thai-cannabis work [^6a]. CBCV shares the formula C19H26O2 with CBDV and Δ8/Δ9-THCV; mass spectrometry alone cannot distinguish them — retention-time/chromatographic resolution is required [^1].

## Thermal-extraction context

{{include includes/boiling-point-vs-device-note.md}}

CBCV is a trace pre-existing constituent released by vapor-pressure-driven evaporation; no compound-specific release or degradation data exist. Device behavior is uncharacterized. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace constituent first co-isolated from Thai-datatype cannabis alongside the varin acids [^5]; measured batches in propyl-rich material report CBCV at trace levels. Values are batch- and report-attached. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBCVA is biosynthesized from CBGVA (propyl branch of the CBGA pathway [^6]) and decarboxylates to CBCV on heating [^5].
