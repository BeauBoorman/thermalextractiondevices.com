CBEA is a pre-existing trace acid expected to decarboxylate to CBE on heating; compound-specific kinetics are unresolved. Device chamber temperature is not sample temperature; the thermodynamic boiling point of the pure compound is not a device setpoint or a sample temperature.

## Cannabis occurrence

Trace acid in aged/oxidized material; measured values are batch- and report-attached. Consult [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch-level measurements.

## Biosynthesis and processing

CBEA forms by oxidative conversion of CBDA (the acid analog of the CBD→CBE pathway demonstrated in plant cultures and mammalian metabolism) [^1][^2], and decarboxylates to CBE on heating.
