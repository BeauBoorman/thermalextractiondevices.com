# Recall Bulletin: Exclusive Brands — MCT Oil in Vape Carts

**Date**: August 27, 2025

**Source**: [CRA Recall Bulletins](https://www.michigan.gov/cra/sections/enforcement-division/recalls)

## Reason for Recall

In its investigation, the CRA discovered through subsequent testing the vapes contained Medium Chain Triglyceride (MCT) oil.

## Affected Products

- Kushy Punch-Vapes (Pineapple Jealousy)

## Affected Licensees

- AU-P-000099 (Exclusive Brands, Ann Arbor)

See the [[licenses/TLIC-0230|Michigan Licensing Overview]] for the full facility list.

## Additional Information

Consumers who purchased Kushy Punch-Vapes (Pineapple Jealousy) produced by Exclusive Brands and sold between January 23, 2025 and May 12, 2025 should check this link and compare the Metrc tag numbers to determine if the product is subject to recall.
