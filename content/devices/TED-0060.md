## Safety Notes

- Dual-cell lithium-ion device charging at up to 16 A: use the supplied USB-C cable and follow the manufacturer's charger guidance [^1].
- Certified according to UL 8139 and DIN EN 60335 (per manufacturer documentation) [^2].

## Warranty & Service

- 2-year warranty, +1 year upon registration (manufacturer stated) [^1].
