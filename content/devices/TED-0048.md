## Usage Notes

- The manufacturer labels the heating system "Hybrid Heating System"; an independent 2024 review instead characterizes the chamber's practical behavior as conduction-dominant and recommends short, slow draws accordingly. Both descriptions are reproduced rather than reconciled — the archive does not resolve manufacturer-vs-reviewer characterizations of heating ratio without a manufacturer-published percentage [^1][^3].

## Warranty & Service

- Flowermate warranty terms vary by model (typically 1-year limited; see the [Smiss manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0016.md) and current official warranty page) [^2].
- Removable 18650 supports user battery replacement.

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

- Per manufacturer guidance: clean the borosilicate mouthpiece and ceramic chamber with the included brush; do not immerse the electronic housing. [^1]

## Safety Notes

- The manufacturer's product page and warranty page do not publish a dedicated hazard/safety document for the V5 Nano beyond the standard removable-18650-battery handling implied by "user-swappable" battery design [^1]; this is a documented absence, not a guarantee of safety. Do not immerse the electronic housing (see Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol) [^1].
