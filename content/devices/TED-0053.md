## Safety & Operating Notes

- The manufacturer requires a **15-minute mandatory cool-down** after use and instructs users **not to unplug the unit during cool-down**; the fan must remain on through the cycle [^1].
- Remove the whip between draws to reduce glass-breakage risk (per manufacturer guidance) [^1].
- The glass-on-glass heating element operates at high temperature; allow full cool-down before handling [^1].

## Warranty & Service

- 3-year manufacturer-defect warranty (glass excluded); 24-hour US replacement documented for the viVape 2 [^1].
- No ongoing repair program documented after the MedBox acquisition; brand inactive since ≈2016–2017 [^1][^2].

## Usage Notes

- **Heat-generation tag judgement call.** No source names the "glass-on-glass heating element" technology explicitly. `resistive` is tagged as the closest defensible value — a coil-in-glass desktop heating element is the same category this archive tags `resistive` elsewhere (VapeXhale's ceramic element); this is an inference, not a manufacturer-stated fact.
- **Operating-mode tag judgement call.** The manufacturer documents a 90-minute auto-off and per-session whip/balloon presets, which is `session` usage; it also matches this archive's own precedent for mains-powered whip/balloon desktop units (Volcano records TED-0034/TED-0035 carry both `session` and `continuous-desktop`), so both are tagged here on the same reasoning — a mains desktop station designed to stay powered through a session, not a battery-portable used in short bursts.
- **Weight/dimension discrepancy.** The primary review states 7.75 × 5.25 × 2.5 in and 1.75 lb [^1]; a retailer listing states 12.00 × 8.00 × 10.00 in and 3.65 lb [^3]. Not resolved — recorded as a discrepancy, not corrected in either direction.
