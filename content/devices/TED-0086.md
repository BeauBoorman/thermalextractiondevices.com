## Safety Notes

- Batteries must be 18650 cells rated ≥20 A continuous discharge; button-top cells will not work; improper batteries can cause equipment damage leading to property or personal injury (manufacturer warning) [^1][^3].

## Safety & Recall Notice

<Aside kind="warning">

**CPSC recall (August 7, 2017).** All Firewood 4 vaporizers sold at Firewoodvapes.com from October 2016 through June 2017 (about 400 units) were recalled by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (recall 17-761) because an electrical short between the battery terminals could cause the battery to vent and catch fire, posing a fire hazard [^5]. The firm received one report of such a short; no injuries were reported. The manufacturer's remedy is a free rework: the battery positive-terminal wire-lead gap is inspected, increased to at least 1 mm where too small, and filled with non-conducting epoxy; fixed units are identifiable by the epoxy-filled gap near the positive battery terminal [^5][^6].

Units purchased between October 17, 2016 and June 27, 2017 that have not received this fix should not be used. Follow the current CPSC and manufacturer recall instructions for the free repair [^5][^6].

Health Canada issued a Canadian counterpart notice for the same defect (RA-64880, October 20, 2017; 15 units sold in Canada; remedy is the same free repair arranged with the manufacturer) — archived as a separate recall entity [^7].

</Aside>
