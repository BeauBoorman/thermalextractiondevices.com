## Safety Notes

- Batteries must be 18650 cells rated ≥20 A continuous discharge; button-top cells will not work; improper batteries can cause equipment damage leading to property or personal injury (manufacturer warning) [^1][^3].

## Warranty & Service

- Field-serviceable by design (user-replaceable battery; parts storefront) [^3][^4].
