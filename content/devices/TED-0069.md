## Safety Notes

- Not for use with tobacco, nicotine-containing e-liquids, or any synthetic nicotine or nicotine substitute (manufacturer warning) [^1].
- Glass assembly is a burn hazard until cooled; avoid liquid ingress into the base [^1].
