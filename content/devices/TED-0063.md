| Property | Specification |

| Heating Method | "Patented combination of convection and conduction heating" (manufacturer description) [^1] |

## Safety Notes

- The bimetallic regulator provides independent temperature control and automatic shutoff (manufacturer safety description) [^1].
- Mains-powered heater: do not leave unattended while hot.

## Warranty & Service

- 2-year warranty (manufacturer stated) [^1].
