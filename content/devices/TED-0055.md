## Safety Notes

- Lithium batteries are volatile; Arizer instructs users to use only official Air MAX batteries and chargers, to match polarity when inserting, and never to store cells fully charged or fully drained for a month or longer [^2].
- Do not leave the heated unit on or unattended; hot glass parts must not contact cold water or extreme temperature change [^2].
