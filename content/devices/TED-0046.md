## Warranty & Service

- 1-year limited warranty (per manufacturer warranty page); batteries excluded from normal degradation [^2].
- No published firmware update process; battery is non-user-replaceable (per manufacturer FAQ) [^2].

## Safety Notes

- FLYTLAB's own FAQ documents only a charging instruction for this model — "Gently connect the TYPE-C cable to the charging port and charge the unit for 1 hr" — and states the battery is non-user-replaceable [^2]. No dedicated hazard/safety document is published for the CTRL 2.0; this is a documented absence, not a guarantee of safety.
- Age-gated sale only ("adults of the legal smoking age") is stated on the product page [^1].

## Usage Notes

- Voltage presets are the only temperature control; the manufacturer does not publish a cartridge temperature setpoint, and voltage is a poor proxy for coil temperature in general (see [Vaporizer Heating Architectures guide](../guides/TGDE-0001.md)).
- The concealed chamber is marketed for discretion and cartridge protection; the enclosure does not alter the cartridge's chemistry.
- **Axis-tag judgement call.** This record is a bare 510 battery, not a complete vaporizer, so heat-generation and delivery-mode axes strictly describe the user-supplied cartridge rather than this device. TREF-0004's axes have no "not applicable" value, so the closest defensible tags are used here: `resistive` (heat_generation) because a 510 cartridge atomizer is a resistive coil the battery drives, and `direct-draw` (delivery_mode) because the assembled unit's only draw path is the cartridge mouthpiece. `on-demand` (operating_mode) is a direct fit — inhale-activation matches the axis definition. Flagged to the integrator as a case where the taxonomy is being stretched to cover a component rather than a complete device.
