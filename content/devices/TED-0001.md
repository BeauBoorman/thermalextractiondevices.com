## Warranty & Service

- 2-year limited warranty on electronics; lifetime warranty on the heating element; battery covered for 1 year; glass components excluded (see [Arizer manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0001.md)) [^4].

## Safety & Recall Notice

<Aside kind="warning">

**CPSC recall (June 18, 2026).** About 5,000 Arizer Solo III portable vaporizers in the **Intergalactic (Black)** color were recalled by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (recall 26-565) because the internal lithium-ion battery can explode or ignite, posing fire and burn hazards [^5]. The firm reported four incidents of battery explosion or ignition. Only devices with serial numbers beginning with the prefixes `M3B1G5`, `M3F4G6`, `M35C43`, `M3PN54`, `M3SR42`, `M38G53`, `M3G576`, or `M3C121` are affected; the serial number is etched on the device bottom and printed on the packaging (UPC 628078802274). Units were sold nationwide and on arizer.com from May 2025 through January 2026.

Consumers should **stop using affected devices immediately** and follow the current manufacturer and regulator recall instructions for replacement and disposal. Recalled units must not be discarded in household trash or general recycling; follow the disposal instructions in the recall notice [^5].

</Aside>

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

- Remove the glass aroma tube and soak the glass components in isopropyl alcohol, rinse with warm water, and dry fully before reuse [^2].
- Do not immerse the main unit body that contains electronics.
