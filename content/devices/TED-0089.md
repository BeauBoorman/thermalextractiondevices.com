## Safety Notes

- Glass mouthpiece: fill with water only per manual guidance (not while attached); take gentler draws if splash occurs [^2].
- Do not use damaged charging equipment; do not disassemble — unauthorized opening voids the warranty [^2].

## Warranty & Service

- 12-month limited hardware warranty; battery and BurstHit 3D Heating Chamber warranted 3 months against abnormal defects (natural degradation excluded); wear-and-tear parts excluded; Thermodyne Systems terms [^2].
