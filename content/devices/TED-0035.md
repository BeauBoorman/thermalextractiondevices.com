## Revision & Variant Notes

- Launched 2019 as the modernized flagship of the Volcano line, per the manufacturer's Storyline: improved airflow control, faster heat-up, touch display, app control with Bluetooth, and the dual inhalation system via valve balloon or tube [^2].
- The [Volcano Classic](../devices/TED-0034.md) (2000, analog) continues in parallel as the simpler model; the Hybrid adds the digital touch display, app control, faster heat-up (≈40 s vs ≈5 min), and whip delivery [^1][^2].
- **2020 chamber-material change (manufacturer-initiated, not a regulator recall):** after reports of metal-on-metal decomposition of the aluminum-alloy heating-element/filling-chamber insert in a limited number of units, Storz & Bickel replaced the aluminum insert with stainless steel; units sold after 2020-01-15 shipped with the stainless steel insert. Documented by third-party vaporizer retailers at the time; no CPSC/regulator recall record was located [^4].
- The "Hybrid" designation denotes the dual balloon + tube systems; the unit is a forced-air convection hot air generator like the Classic, not a conduction/convection hybrid [^1][^2].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Official guidance: allow the filling chamber, cap, and Tube Kit to cool before disassembly; the unit has hot surfaces [^3][^5].
- The consumer VOLCANO HYBRID is not a certified medical device. Only the VOLCANO MEDIC 2 is certified under EU MDR (TÜV SÜD, July 2023) [^2].
