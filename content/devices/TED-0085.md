## Safety Notes

- Battery must be a flat-top 18650 rated ≥20 A continuous discharge; button-top cells will not work, and under-rated cells can cause equipment damage, property damage, or personal injury (manufacturer warning) [^1][^3].
- Use only chargers intended for 18650 cells with over-charge protection [^3].

## Warranty & Service

- Field-serviceable design with user-replaceable battery and oven lid; parts sold on the storefront [^1][^4].
