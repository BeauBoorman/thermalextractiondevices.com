## Safety Notes

- Five rapid button-presses power the battery on/off; the leftmost LED blinking red 5 times signals low battery (manufacturer FAQ) [^2].
- Quartz tank is a burn hazard until cooled; no exposed nail in normal use [^1].

## Warranty & Service
