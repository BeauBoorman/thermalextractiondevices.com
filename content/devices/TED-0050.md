## Warranty & Service

- 1-year limited warranty on electronics (batteries excluded from normal degradation), per TopGreen support policy [^3].

## Safety Notes

- The official product-detail listing documents a "Safety Power Duration" auto-shutoff feature, selectable at 5 or 10 minutes, as the device's only named safety mechanism [^4]. No further hazard/safety document is published; this is a documented absence, not a guarantee of safety.

## Usage Notes

- **Heat-generation tag judgement call.** The official product-detail listing describes "ceramic with embedded heating element" and a "full ceramic conduction oven chamber" [^4]. `resistive` is tagged as the closest defensible value for an embedded ceramic heating element, consistent with this archive's precedent (VapeXhale's ceramic element is tagged `resistive`); the listing does not use the word "resistive" itself.
