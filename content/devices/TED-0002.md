## Safety Notes

- DynaVap's FAQ instructs users to stop heating "if more than 6 seconds pass without hearing the click" with a torch, and never to apply heat past the click — doing so can permanently damage the cap [^2].
- The Captive Cap and tip reach the device's highest temperatures immediately after heating; DynaVap's guidance is to wait for the audible cool-down click before handling the cap or emptying the bowl [^2].
- Use only butane-fueled jet lighters or a DynaVap-compatible induction heater as the heat source; the device carries no batteries or mains connection and has no built-in thermal cutoff [^2].

## Warranty & Service

- DynaVap offers a limited lifetime warranty on functional metal/components and on replacement parts; caps, CCDs, O-rings, and accessories are excluded [^5].
- Device is fully user-serviceable and disassembles without tools.

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

1. **Disassembly**: Remove Captive Cap, tip, condenser, and fluoroelastomer o-rings.
2. **Cleaning**: Soak stainless steel stem, tip, and condenser in isopropyl alcohol. **Do not soak the Captive Cap** in alcohol or water; DynaVap recommends cleaning the cap with a swab, not soaking [^6].
3. **Reassembly**: Lubricate o-rings with DynaWax or hemp oil and reassemble condenser into stem body.
