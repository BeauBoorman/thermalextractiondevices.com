## Revision & Variant Notes

- The Classic is the original VOLCANO: development began 1996, the VOLCANO patent for the detachable balloon was granted in 1999, and the first units reached the market in 2000 — described by the manufacturer as "the first fully functional herbal hot air generator" [^2].
- Siblings in the Volcano family: VOLCANO DIGIT (2007–2020, discontinued — the first electronically controlled model, with digital temperature display) and VOLCANO HYBRID (2019, current — adds dual inhalation via valve balloon or tube/whip) [^2][^3].
- The Classic remains the analog-control model; the manufacturer markets it as "unlimited sessions" and "engineered for longevity" [^1].
- Anniversary editions: VOLCANO CLASSIC Gold Edition (2020, 20th anniversary) and VOLCANO CLASSIC 25 Years Edition (2025, green with gold-plated accents) [^2].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Official quick-start cautions: the unit has a hot surface; never leave the filling chamber on the VOLCANO after use [^5].
- The consumer VOLCANO CLASSIC is not a certified medical device. Only the VOLCANO MEDIC 2 is certified under EU MDR (TÜV SÜD, July 2023); the earlier VOLCANO MEDIC (2010–2019) is discontinued [^2][^3].
