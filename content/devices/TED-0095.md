## Safety Notes

- Water-chamber device: follow filling guidance; keep liquid away from the electronics base [^1].
- Heated quartz insert is a burn hazard until cooled [^1].

## Warranty & Service

- 1-year warranty on the Switch GO battery, charger, and electrical components [^1].
