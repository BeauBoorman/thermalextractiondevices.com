# PAX Era Pro (TED-0090)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer | [PAX Labs, Inc.](../manufacturers/TMFR-0022.md) |
| Family | Era oil-pod system (Pro tier) |
| Part Number | PAX does not publish a discrete alphanumeric SKU on its pages; the product is identified by the model designation "Era Pro" [^1] |
| Form Factor | Pen-style handheld in anodized aluminum, which "evenly distribute[s] the heat generated by the battery" [^3] |
| Heating Method | Pod-based conduction heating of cannabis-oil extract pods (proprietary Era pod format; not 510-threaded) [^3][^4] |
| Heat Generation | Electric pod heater; general `resistive` classification per archive convention [^3] |
| Operation Mode | On-demand draw with closed-loop temperature control; instant draw when raised to the lips [^1][^3] |
| Temperature Control | Four temperature modes between 520 °F and 790 °F, cycled via Pop-and-Click pod interaction; app-based control via Bluetooth [^2][^3] |
| Features | Haptic vibration; longer battery life and 50 % improved Bluetooth range over the classic Era; session metadata and pod-ID transparency via app [^1] |
| Power Source | Internal rechargeable battery (capacity not published) [^1] |
| Charging | USB-C; ≈45-minute charge time; four LED petals indicate charge in 25 % steps [^4] |
| Battery Indication | Shake to display battery level; red LED at ≤15 % remaining [^4] |
| Compatible Media | PAX Era oil pods (live rosin, diamonds, high-purity THC lines) — closed pod system [^5] |

## Safety Notes

- Closed-pod system: use only genuine PAX Era pods; pod authenticity/ID verification is an app feature [^1][^5].
- Lithium-ion device: ≈45-min USB-C charge; follow charging guidance [^4].

## Warranty & Service

- Covered under the PAX warranty-policy family (Era tier; see the [PAX manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0022.md) — Era Pro-specific terms live in the policy page) [^6].
