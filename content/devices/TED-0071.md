## Safety Notes

- Charge for 2 hours before first use; do not use while charging; do not charge overnight or unattended (manufacturer manual guidance) [^1].
- Run a maximum-temperature 5-minute cycle before first use to burn off manufacturing residue (manufacturer guidance) [^1].
