## Warranty & Service

- Mig Vapor carried a 90-day manufacturer-defect warranty across its line (per period retailer policy records); the brand is inactive and no ongoing warranty service exists [^2].

## Safety & Certification Notes

- No FCC/CE/RoHS certification filings and no public recalls were located for Mig Vapor devices (documented absence, not a guarantee) [^2].

## Usage Notes

- **Heat-generation tag judgement call.** No source names the Khan's ceramic-chamber element technology explicitly. `resistive` is tagged as the closest defensible value, consistent with this archive's precedent for ceramic heating chambers (VapeXhale's ceramic element is tagged `resistive`); this is an inference, not a manufacturer-stated fact.
- **Battery-capacity discrepancy.** This record's primary sources state a 2500 mAh battery [^1]; a third-party retailer listing titles the kit "2200 mAh" [^4]. Not resolved — recorded as a discrepancy, not corrected in either direction.
