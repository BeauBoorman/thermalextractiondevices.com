## Warranty & Service

- 12-month hardware warranty; 3-month battery warranty; wear parts (mouthpiece, screens, tools, brush, USB-C cable) excluded [^1].
- Only the manufacturer / trained service staff may open the device; user disassembly voids warranty [^1].
- Device is **not** a medical device and not a smoking-cessation tool (per manual) [^1].

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

- Per the official manual: clean the mouthpiece, screens, and ceramic filter with the included brush; keep charging surfaces clean; do not charge on soft or insulating surfaces [^1].

## Safety Notes

- Manual cautions: keep away from children, avoid use near flammable materials, and do not use damaged cables or chargers; users with lung or respiratory disease are advised against vapor devices (per manual) [^1].

## Usage Notes

- **Heat-generation tag judgement call.** The manual documents "a dual heating mechanism to heat the bottom and sides of your bowl evenly" in an anodized-aluminum chamber but does not name the element technology explicitly. `resistive` is tagged as the closest defensible value — every dual/embedded heating-chamber design in this archive's own taxonomy examples (E-Nano ceramic rod, VapeXhale ceramic element) resolves to `resistive` — but this is an inference from the manual's description, not a manufacturer-stated fact, and is flagged here accordingly [^4].
