## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |

## Recall Context

- The Solo II MAX is the designated replacement unit for the 2025 CPSC recall of the Solo II (lithium-ion battery fire/burn hazard; ≈5,460 US units) — affected consumers were offered a free Solo II MAX [^4].

## Safety Notes

- Lithium batteries are volatile; Arizer instructs users to use only official Solo II MAX batteries and chargers and never to drop the device or batteries [^2].
- Do not leave the heated unit on or unattended; hot glass parts must not contact cold water or extreme temperature change [^2].

## Warranty & Service

- Arizer's general warranty policy applies (see [Arizer manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0001.md)); no Solo II MAX-specific duration is published on the product page [^1][^5].
