## Safety Notes

- Desktop mains-powered heater: do not leave the unit turned on and unattended (Arizer support guidance) [^2].
- Replaceable air-intake filter cartridge maintains the isolated air path; follow the manufacturer's cartridge replacement guidance [^1].

## Warranty & Service

- Arizer's general warranty policy applies (see [Arizer manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0001.md)); the manufacturer's US site states the Extreme Q desktop carries a 3-year warranty — no XQ2-specific duration is published on the product page [^1][^3].
