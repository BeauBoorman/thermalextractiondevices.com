## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer | [Boundless Technology](../manufacturers/TMFR-0026.md) |
| Family | Terp Pen concentrate line (Version II) |
| Part Number | Boundless does not publish a discrete alphanumeric SKU on the product page; the product is identified by the model designation "Terp Pen Version II" [^1] |

| Heat Generation | Electric coil; general `resistive` classification per archive convention [^1] |
| Operation Mode | On-demand, draw-activated concentrate vaping [^1] |

## Safety Notes

- Exposed heated coil: the tip is a burn hazard during and immediately after use; magnetic cap when idle [^1].
- Manufacturer publishes Prop 65 cannabis-risk warnings and adult-use-only disclaimers storefront-wide [^3].
