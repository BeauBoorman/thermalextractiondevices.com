## Safety Notes

- Heated cap and tip are burn hazards until cooled; the click signals temperature, not safety [^1].
- XL configuration lengthens the vapor path; the isolated mouthpiece reduces stem heat at the lips (per manufacturer description) [^3].

## Warranty & Service
