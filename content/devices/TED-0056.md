## Safety Notes

- Lithium batteries are volatile; use only official Air SE batteries and chargers; never throw the batteries away or place them in a battery collection box (Arizer's disposal guidance is followed instead) [^2].
- Warning — hot parts: handle hot and glass parts with care; do not place hot parts on non-heat-safe surfaces; allow the heating unit to cool before pocketing or storing [^2].
- Not for use with e-liquids, tobacco, or tobacco products [^2].
