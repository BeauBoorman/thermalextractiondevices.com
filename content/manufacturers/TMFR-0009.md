## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Methods**: Conduction (pens and Boost line), electromagnetic induction (Switch line), with closed-loop temperature sensing introduced on Boost EVO (embedded sensor) and Switch² (IR sensor) [^1][^2].
- **Temperature Control**: Preset-based with app control on current models (250–650 °F on Switch² / Switch GO; 500–750 °F six-step presets on Boost EVO) [^1][^2].
- **Firmware**: App-updatable on Boost EVO, Switch², and Switch GO (Bluetooth); older models are button-only [^1][^2].
- **Materials**: Quartz eChambers/inserts (current models); ceramic induction cups (Switch OG); sapphire options on several models [^1].
- **Safety**: Over-temperature/voltage/current/short-circuit/reverse-polarity protection cited in current-model manuals; 15-minute auto shutoff on Switch² [^2].
