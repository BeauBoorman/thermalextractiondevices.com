# 7th Floor Vapes

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | 7th Floor Vapes / Elev8 Brands ("ELEV8 LIFE BRANDS (7TH FLOOR - ELEV8 PREMIER)" per brand listing) |
| Manufacturing | Assembled, crafted, and tested in Colorado Springs, Colorado, USA ("at the foot of Pikes Peak") |
| Origin | The Silver Surfer platform born 2004 |

## Official Product Families

- **Desktop Whip Units**: Silver Surfer (SSV, 2004–present), Da Buddha (DBV) [^1][^2].
- **Later Platforms**: DBV3, Life Saber Vape (documented in the storefront lineup; not yet modeled) [^2].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Ceramic heating element, fully encased and kept away from the air to eliminate oxidation (manufacturer claims first-in-industry for this heater type); convection hot-air extraction [^1].
- **Control Model**: Hand-blown glass temperature knobs without numbered settings — users mark their own "sweet spot" [^1].
- **Materials**: 1/4-inch-thick aircraft-grade aluminum housings; glass-on-glass spherical joints; food-grade vinyl whip tubing [^1].
- **Multi-Use**: Dry herb, concentrates (quartz dab dish), and aroma diffusion (aroma top) on one unit [^1].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- After-warranty repairs are offered as easy/user-serviceable (manufacturer positioning); terms on the warranty page [^1][^3].
