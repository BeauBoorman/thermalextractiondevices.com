# Tronian (Thermodyne Systems brand)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Tronian is a brand of **Thermodyne Systems**, with Canadian and German entities identified in official manuals [^1][^2] |
| Headquarters | Toronto, Canada; Stuttgart, Germany (dual addresses on official manuals and the official store locator) [^1][^2] |
| Operating Principle | Consumer vapor hardware — 510 cartridge batteries, disposable oil pens, dry-herb portables, wax pens, and vapor cups |
| Foundation Year | Brand founded ≈2018 (per TVape brand page; not dated on tronian.com — treated as secondary) [^3] |

## Official Product Families

- **510-Thread Batteries**: Pitron (V1)/Pitron V2, Tautron, Nutron (V1)/Nutron V2, Rhotron, Rhotron Pro. [^1][^4]
- **Disposable Oil Pens**: Alfatron. [^1]
- **Dry-Herb Portables**: Milatron (the brand's first dry-herb device). [^1][^5]
- **Concentrates**: Gammatron (wax pen), Omegatron (vapor cup with fan-assisted glass chamber). [^1]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Conduction via attached 510 cartridges (battery line); hybrid convection-dominant heating (Milatron, per enthusiast analysis — the official manual does not label the architecture); twisted Kanthal coil (Gammatron); glass-core heating element with fan assist (Omegatron). [^1][^5]
- **Power Sources**: Internal lithium-ion batteries (e.g., Milatron 2300 mAh; 5 V DC / 2 A USB-C input); charging via micro-USB on older 510 models and USB-C on newer ones. [^1]
- **Temperature Control**: Digital, with OLED display on the Milatron (160–240 °C evaporation range per manual); fixed-voltage presets on 510 batteries; fully adjustable voltage (1.6–4.2 V in 0.1 V steps) on the Rhotron Pro. [^1][^5]
- **Device Status**: Manuals state devices are **not medical devices** and not smoking-cessation tools. [^1]

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 12-month limited hardware warranty; 3-month battery warranty (consistent across manuals). [^1]
- **Service & Repair**: Only the manufacturer / trained service staff may open devices; user disassembly voids warranty; manuals include counterfeiting warnings. [^1]
- **Recalls**: No formal recalls or manufacturer field safety notices located (absence of evidence, not a guarantee). [^1]
