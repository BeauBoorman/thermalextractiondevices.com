# TopGreen Technology Co., Ltd. (XMAX)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | TopGreen Technology Co., Ltd. (Shenzhen); North American HQ is TopGreen US Corp (Southern California) [^1] |
| Headquarters | Shenzhen, Guangdong, China (Bao'an District, Fuhai Street); TopGreen US Corp in Southern California [^1][^2] |
| Operating Principle | Vertically integrated vapor hardware manufacturer — in-house R&D, production, OEM/ODM; consumer brands XMAX, XVape, XLux |
| Foundation Year | 2000 (per official About page: "Established 2000 Year") [^1] |

**Brand relationship.** XMAX is TopGreen's primary consumer brand; XVape is a sibling consumer brand and XLux a premium offshoot of the same manufacturer. The official site lists both XMAX and XVape product lines under "TOPGREEN US CORPORATION" with primary services "XMAX/XVAPE Aromatherapy Diffuser" [^1]. XMAX and XVape are kept as distinct brand subjects on this archive ([XVape record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0018.md)).

## Official Product Families

- **XMAX V-Series dry-herb portables**: V2, V2 Pro, II Pro, V3 Pro, V3 Nano, Ace. [^1][^2]
- **XMAX Starry series**: Starry v1, Starry 3.0, Starry 4. [^1]
- **XMAX concentrate devices**: Qomo (dab rig), V-ONE / V-ONE Plus, Riggo (dual e-nail/pipe), Daboo (nectar collector). [^1]
- **XLux (premium offshoot)**: Roffu convection portable. [^2]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Conduction ceramic ovens on early V-series; hybrid convection-dominant heating with a stainless-steel oven on the V3 Pro; convection on the XLux Roffu. [^2]
- **Power Sources**: Replaceable 18650 cells on V-series portables (e.g., V3 Pro); USB-C charging on current models. [^2]
- **Temperature Control**: Digital, with 100–220 °C range on the V3 Pro (per manufacturer documentation); presets on Starry models. [^2]

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: Typically 1-year limited warranty on electronics (batteries excluded from normal degradation); some models carry 2-year coverage; concentrate atomizers 6 months (per official support pages; verify the current model terms before relying on them). [^2][^3]
- **Service & Repair**: RMA requires serial number + scratch-off security code and proof of purchase; opening the device voids warranty. [^3]
- **Recalls**: No public CPSC / EU RAPEX recalls found as of the 2026-08-08 verification pass. [^2]
