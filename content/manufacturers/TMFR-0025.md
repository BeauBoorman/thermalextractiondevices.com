{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Healthy Rips by Green Curative, Inc. (per official product-launch announcement) |
| Operating Principle | "Dry Herb Extraction (DHE)" devices — the manufacturer's term for convection vaporization |

## Official Product Families

- **Current Portables**: Fury Edge (2019), Rogue (2021) [^3][^4].
- **Legacy**: Fury, Fury 2 (documented in the official launch lineage) [^4].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Convection heating with isolated air path (per product and manual documentation) [^1][^2].
- **Control Model**: OLED digital screens, three-press power control, °F/°C switching, flip display [^1][^2].
- **Batteries**: Internal lithium-polymer cells (2300 mAh Fury Edge, 3000 mAh Rogue) [^1][^2].
