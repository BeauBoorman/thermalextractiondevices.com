## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Puffco (US; New York-based per official site contact information; see Sources) |
| Operating Principle | Concentrate-first smart rigs and vaporizers with app customization (see Sources) |

## Official Product Families

- **Smart E-Rigs**: Peak Pro 3DXL (current flagship) [^1].
- **Portables / Other**: Proxy, Plus, Vision electronic dab tools — documented on the official site (not yet modeled) [^3].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Electronic heated chambers (ceramic bowl e-rigs) with real-time temperature control [^1].
- **Control Model**: Single-button interface with four customizable heat profiles; Bluetooth customization via the Puffco Connect app [^1].
- **Delivery**: Water-filtered glass assemblies on e-rigs (laser-cut perc slots) [^1].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- Warranty terms are published in the support center rather than product pages (see support articles) [^2].
