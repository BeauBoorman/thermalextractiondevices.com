# Firewood Aromatech

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Firewood Aromatech — handmade in the USA by an artisan named Marc; a new vaporizer is released each year, sometimes incremental, sometimes a complete overhaul |
| Operating Principle | Speed, portability, discretion, aesthetics, value, and taste (manufacturer's own summary) |

## Official Product Families

- **Current**: Firewood 12 (walnut body, quartz-glass oven, hybrid heating) [^1].
- **Documented lineage**: Firewood 4 through 11 — wood/ceramic/glass construction with on-demand convection and later hybrid heating [^2][^4].
- **Firewood 4 (2017)**: CPSC recall (August 7, 2017; ≈400 units) — an electrical short between the battery terminals could cause the battery to vent and catch fire; remedy was a free rework increasing the battery-terminal gap to ≥1 mm and filling it with non-conducting epoxy [^5].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Hybrid convection/conduction on current models ("slightly more convection and less conduction" in the 12); earlier generations on-demand convection [^1][^4].
- **Batteries**: User-replaceable flat-top 18650 cells rated ≥20 A continuous discharge (button-top cells do not work); internal USB-C chargers [^1][^2].
- **Materials**: Black Walnut bodies; ovens of borosilicate (FW4), ceramic/stainless (FW7), or quartz glass (FW12); 100 % natural Tung Oil and Citrus Solvent finish [^1][^2].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- Field-serviceable by design: user-replaceable batteries and oven/battery lids; parts sold on the storefront [^1][^3].
