# PAX Labs, Inc.

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | PAX Labs, Inc. (US; Delaware-incorporated per third-party business records) [^3] |
| Headquarters | San Francisco, California, USA (per official site contact information) [^3] |
| Operating Principle | "All heat, never burn your flower" — heated-flower vaporization (manufacturer phrase) [^1] |
| Lineage | The PAX product line descends from the Pax device by Ploom (2012); PAX Labs was spun out as an independent company (per official company history page) [^3] |

## Official Product Families

- **Current Portables**: PAX Plus (dual-use flower + concentrate), PAX MINI (flower) [^1][^2].
- **Legacy Portables** (documented for lineage): PAX 2, PAX 3, PAX Era (oil pods), original Pax by Ploom [^2].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Heated-oven conduction with patented temperature control (the product pages describe "patented temperature control" without publishing a conduction/convection split) [^1][^2].
- **Control Model**: App-free Experience Modes on current models (Stealth, Efficiency, Flavor, Boost), replacing the app-driven presets of PAX 3 [^1].
- **Oven**: Anodized-aluminum-shell devices with removable oven screens; Half Pack Oven Lid accessory for reduced loads [^1].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: "Limited Warranty" stated on the product page; duration and terms are published in the warranty policy rather than the product listing [^1][^3].
