## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Methods**: Conduction (early pens and Dash), hybrid conduction + convection (Elite II, Dash+), ceramic/quarts conduction (wax and e-rig lines) [^1].
- **Temperature Control**: Fixed presets on budget models; digital ±1 °F/°C control on Elite II and Dash+; voltage presets on pens [^1].
- **Firmware**: Wi-Fi firmware updating claimed for Elite II and Hyer; most models have no user-updatable firmware [^1].
- **Certifications**: FCC compliance likely but no public FCC IDs located; the Elite II has an Australian TGA/ARTG listing (Entry 526764) via Grenco Medical [^2][^3].
- **Recalls / Safety**: None known for G Pen-branded devices [^3].
