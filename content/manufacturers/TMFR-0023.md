# DaVinci Vaporizers

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | DaVinci Vaporizers brand; the storefront is operated by Mercury One, Inc. (per official site checkout/footer documentation) [^4] |
| Headquarters | USA (per official site; Las Vegas, Nevada per third-party business records) [^4] |
| Operating Principle | "Clean First" technology — zirconia-ceramic and glass air paths, removable batteries [^1][^2] |

## Official Product Families

- **Current Dry-Herb Portables**: IQ3, IQ2, IQC, MIQRO-C; ARTIQ cartridge vaporizer [^3].
- **Legacy**: IQ, MIQRO (documented in the manufacturer's owner's-manual archive) [^3].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: "Heat Not Burn" glass-lined conduction ovens (manufacturer phrase) [^1][^2].
- **Air Path**: Airtight zirconia-ceramic and glass vapor path on IQ-tier models (MIQRO-C mouthpiece is silicone per the comparison table) [^1][^2].
- **Batteries**: Removable, rechargeable lithium-ion cells (18650 on IQ-tier, 18350 on MIQRO-C) [^1][^2].
- **Control Model**: On-device LED display plus Bluetooth app with Smart Paths (staged temperature profiles) [^1].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 10 years on IQ2, 5 years on IQC and MIQRO-C (per product pages; the MIQRO-C comparison table shows conflicting values — see device records) [^1][^2][^3].
