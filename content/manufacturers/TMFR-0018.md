# XVape (TopGreen Technology brand)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | XVape is a consumer brand of TopGreen Technology Co., Ltd.; North American HQ is TopGreen US Corp (Southern California) [^1] |
| Headquarters | Shenzhen, Guangdong, China; TopGreen US Corp in Southern California [^1] |
| Operating Principle | Consumer vapor hardware — dry-herb portables, convection portables, and concentrate e-rigs |
| Foundation Year | TopGreen Technology established 2000; XVape brand appears on official TopGreen channels alongside XMAX [^1] |

**Brand relationship.** XVape and XMAX are distinct consumer brands of the same manufacturer (TopGreen Technology). The official TopGreen site carries both lines ("Primary services: XMAX/XVAPE Aromatherapy Diffuser") [^1]. This archive keeps them as separate brand subjects; see the [XMAX record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0017.md).

## Official Product Families

- **Dry-herb portables**: XVape Vital (2016-era, fixed battery), XVape Avant, XVape Aria / Aria+. [^1][^2]
- **Convection portable**: XVape Fog Pro (OLED, 2-in-1 dry herb and wax). [^1]
- **Concentrate e-rigs**: XVape Vista Mini / Vista Mini 2, XVape V-ONE 2.0 (wax pen). [^1][^2]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Hybrid conduction (Aria, marketed 2-in-1 dry herb and wax); convection (Fog Pro); ceramic coil e-rigs (Vista Mini 2, quartz coil). [^1][^2]
- **Power Sources**: Internal lithium-ion batteries; USB-C on current models. [^2]
- **Airpath Wording**: Official listings use the phrase "100% isolated airflow" (e.g., Aria in Lake Blue). Per archive convention this is reproduced as a marketing claim and is not an independently verified engineering fact. [^1]

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: Typically 1-year limited warranty on electronics (per TopGreen support pages; batteries excluded from normal degradation). [^2]
- **Service & Repair**: RMA via authorized retailer or TopGreen US (xvapeusa.com); serial + security code required. [^2]
