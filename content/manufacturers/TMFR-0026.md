## Official Product Families

- **Dry-Herb Portables**: CFC 2.0 (conduction compact), CFX / CFX+ (hybrid heater with TFT screen) [^1][^2].
- **Concentrate Pens**: Terp Pen, Terp Pen Spectrum (dab pens) [^1].
- Legacy models (CFV, CF) documented in the storefront lineage [^1].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Conduction (CFC 2.0); hybrid conduction + convection with two independent heaters (CFX); heated-coil concentrate pens (Terp Pen line) [^1][^2].
- **Chambers**: Ceramic chambers; "fully isolated air-path" claimed on CFX (marketing phrase, not independently verified here) [^2].
- **Safety Posture**: Prop 65 cannabis warning and "not FDA approved / adult use" disclaimers published storefront-wide [^3].
