# Vaporfection International, Inc.

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Vaporfection International, Inc. [^1] |
| Headquarters | Florida, USA [^1] |
| Operating Principle | Forced-air convection desktop and portable vaporizers with glass vapor paths (Vapor Glass™) and touchscreen control (Vapor Touch™) |
| Foundation Year | 2003 per unpublished notes; **not independently confirmed** — the earliest primary evidence located is the 2011 patent application and the viVape pre-order (2011) [^1][^4] |
| Status | Acquired by **MedBox, Inc.** (purchase agreement 22 Mar 2013, closed 1 Apr 2013, ≈$7.6M assumed value; wholly owned subsidiary) [^1][^2]; brand dormant since ≈2016–2017 |

## Official Product Families

- **viVape Series (desktop)**: viVape (Gen 1, ≈2011), viVape 2 (May 2014). [^3]
- **miVape Series (portable)**: miVape (c. mid-2014). [^3]
- **Accessories**: Aqua Vape water-filtration attachment. [^3]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Forced-air convection over a glass-on-glass heating element (viVape series); battery convection with a glass spiral air path and removable quartz cups (miVape). [^3]
- **Power Sources**: AC mains, dual-voltage 110/240 V (viVape series); removable/replaceable lithium-ion cell (miVape). [^3]
- **Temperature Control**: Vapor Touch™ touchscreen (viVape series, 100–430 °F per Medical Jane); digital buttons with LCD (miVape). [^3]
- **IP**: Vapor Glass™ / Vapor Touch™ patents assigned to Vaporfection International, Inc. (filed 2011, granted 2014, inventor Amir Yomtov) [^4].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 3-year manufacturer-defect warranty on viVape units (glass excluded); miVape documented at 6-month battery-only warranty. [^3]
- **Service & Repair**: No ongoing repair program documented post-acquisition; brand inactive since ≈2016–2017. [^3]
