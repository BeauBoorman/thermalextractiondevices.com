# Mig Vapor LLC

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Mig Vapor LLC [^1] |
| Headquarters | Pompano Beach, Florida, USA [^1] |
| Operating Principle | Consumer vapor hardware — dry-herb portables, concentrate pens, oil/cartridge devices, e-cigarette batteries and tanks |
| Foundation Year | 2013 (per business-profile records) [^1] |
| Status | Operations discontinued circa 2020–2021; VaporFi is the official home / exclusive distributor of Mig Vapor products (VaporFi blog, live page) [^2] |

<Aside kind="note">

Mig Vapor's direct support infrastructure is inactive and the brand's official site is no longer maintained. Specifications on this archive for Mig Vapor devices therefore derive from retailer and reviewer documentation and are labeled as third-party where no official manual was located.

</Aside>

## Official Product Families

- **Dry-herb portables**: Herb-E, Torpedo, DRAY, The FOUR, Khan. [^3]
- **Concentrate pens**: Sol (2-in-1), Wasp Wax Air, Herb-E Seven Micro Wax, Brain Fogger (510 atomizer). [^3]
- **Oil / cartridge devices**: Bug RX, Trap RX. [^3]
- **E-cigarette line**: Mig 21 Clear Fusion kits, eVod/510 batteries, NEO and Minion mods, Sub Herb 510 tanks. [^3]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Conduction ceramic chambers on most dry-herb units; the DRAY used a convection quartz chamber; the Khan added a glass bubbler; 510 tanks used ceramic-plate heating. [^3]
- **Power Sources**: Internal lithium-polymer batteries (e.g., Khan 2500 mAh); micro-USB charging. [^3]
- **Certifications**: No FCC/CE/RoHS certification filings or public recalls were located (documented absence; not a guarantee). [^3]

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 90-day manufacturer defect warranty across the line (per period retailer policy records). [^3]
- **Service & Repair**: With the brand inactive, warranty service is no longer offered directly; legacy inventory is sold via VaporFi and wholesale distributors. [^2][^3]
