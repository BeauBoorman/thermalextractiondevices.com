# Utillian

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Utillian is a brand of **Thermodyne Systems** — the limited warranty in official manuals "only applies to devices manufactured by Thermodyne Systems™ that can be identified by the 'UTILLIAN' trademark" [^3] |
| Headquarters | Toronto, Canada; Stuttgart, Germany (Thermodyne dual addresses; see the [Tronian record](TMFR-0015.md) for the shared entity) [^1][^2] |
| Brand Sibling | [Tronian (Thermodyne Systems brand)](TMFR-0015.md) — same manufacturer, separate consumer brand [^3] |

## Official Product Families

- **Numbered Dry-Herb Portables**: 620, 621 (convection helix chamber), 722; wax canister dual-use on the 62x series [^1][^4].
- **Glass-Path Flagships**: Utillian 8 / 8 V2 (BurstHit 3D Heating Chamber, water-capable glass mouthpiece) [^2][^3].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Convection helix chamber (621) preventing clogging with cleanable airpath; BurstHit 3D Heating Chamber (8 V2) [^1][^2].
- **Temperature Control**: Precise set-point control 160–220 °C on the 621; four temperature levels + Boost mode (245–330 °C) on the 8 V2 [^1][^2].
- **Power**: 2300 mAh battery (621) with USB-C 1.8 A charging; 3150 mAh battery (8 V2), 5 V DC input [^1][^2].
- **Device Status**: Not medical devices; not for illegal, medicinal, or unregulated substances (manual statements) [^1][^2].

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 12-month limited hardware warranty against defects; 3-month battery and BurstHit chamber warranty; wear-and-tear parts excluded; warranty does not restart after a claim [^1][^2][^3].
