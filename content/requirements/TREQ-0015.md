# Sample Homogeneity

**Citation**: CRA Lab Technical Guidance 5.2 §5.3

Laboratories must ensure sample homogeneity before analysis. For flower, a representative sample from throughout the batch must be homogenized by grinding. For concentrates and infused products, standard laboratory homogenization techniques apply.
