# Pesticide Screening

**Citation**: CRA Lab Technical Guidance 5.2 §4.5; R 420.304(1)(e)

All marijuana products must be screened for pesticides listed in the CRA's mandatory pesticide panel. The action limit for most analytes is 0.1 ppm, though selected compounds have higher or lower limits. Michigan's pesticide list is one of the more comprehensive state panels.
