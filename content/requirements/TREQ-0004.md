# Potency / Cannabinoid Testing

**Citation**: CRA Lab Technical Guidance 5.2 §4.1; R 420.304

All marijuana products must be tested for cannabinoid potency. The CRA requires testing for: THCA, Delta-9-THC, CBDA, CBD, CBN, CBGA, CBG, Delta-8-THC (when present), and THCV (when present). Total THC is calculated as: THCA × 0.877 + Delta-9-THC.

<Aside kind="info">

Michigan requires delta-8-THC testing when the cannabinoid is detected above the LOQ. This is a notable difference from several other states that do not explicitly mandate delta-8 quantification.

</Aside>
