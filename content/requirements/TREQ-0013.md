# Vitamin E Acetate

**Citation**: CRA Lab Technical Guidance 5.2 §4.13; R 420.304(1)(k)

Marijuana vapor products (vape cartridges) must be tested for vitamin E acetate. Action limit is not detected. This requirement was added following the 2019 EVALI outbreak.

## Action Limits

| Analyte | Class | Action Limit | Unit | Matrix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vitamin E Acetate | adulterant | Not detected | — | Vapor products |
