# Foreign Matter Inspection

**Citation**: CRA Lab Technical Guidance 5.2 §4.9; R 420.304(1)(i)

All marijuana products must be visually inspected for foreign matter (hair, insects, packaging debris, etc.). No numeric action limit; this is a qualitative pass/fail inspection.


<Aside kind="tip">

All action limits are sourced from the CRA Technical Guidance for Laboratories, Version 5.2 (September 2024). Verify against the current version for regulatory compliance.

</Aside>
