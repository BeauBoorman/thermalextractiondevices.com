# CCB Public Health and Safety Bulletins (WP-JSON)

<Aside kind="info">

Public Health and Safety Bulletins are published by the Nevada Cannabis Compliance Board. The Board's terminology is preserved; bulletins are not relabeled as recalls unless the Board does so.

</Aside>

## Dataset Notes

Machine-readable advisory posts from the CCB WordPress REST API: bulletin id/date/title, affected items (product, batch/lot), retail locations, consumer guidance, and the CDC/agency links the Board cites. Street addresses inside retail-location lines are excluded from generated pages.
