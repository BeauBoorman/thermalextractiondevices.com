# Health Canada Recall Sweep (Device-Relevant)

The 2026-09-06 sweep of the Health Canada Recalls and Safety Alerts
open-data feed for device-relevant recall events, normalized into
`data/health-canada/recall-sweep.json`. The feed is published daily under
the Open Government Licence – Canada; the archived notice pages are the
source of record for the modeled entities.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Feed rows at retrieval | 34,052 |
| Cannabis subcategory rows | 98 |
| Consumer product safety rows | 5,194 |
| Device-relevant candidates selected | 11 |
| Modeled recall entities | 4 (`TRCL-0013`–`TRCL-0016`) |
| Retrieval date | 2026-09-06 |
| Primary authority | Health Canada |
| Licence | Open Government Licence – Canada |

## Modeled entities

- Firewood 4 portable vaporizers, 2017 (`TRCL-0013`; Canadian counterpart of
  CPSC 17-761)
- HEXO all-in-one disposable cannabis vape pens, 2021 (`TRCL-0014`;
  cannabis-product recall with a device-level hardware cause)
- Arizer Solo II vaporizer, 2025 (`TRCL-0015`; Canadian counterpart of the
  CPSC notice)
- Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black), 2026 (`TRCL-0016`; Health
  Canada / CPSC joint recall)

## Not modeled

- Grower's Edge Deluxe/Commercial vaporizers (2020) and Wismec Exo Skeleton
  (2018): no archive entities exist for these manufacturers.
- Cannabis-subcategory recalls naming extract/pre-roll/flower products
  without device-hardware scope: out of device-recall graph scope; the full
  cannabis feed remains a bulk-ingestion candidate for the Canada
  jurisdiction lane.

## Limitation

The feed rows are discovery-level (title, organization, issue, category,
date). Unit counts, affected lots, incident counts, and company roles were
taken from the archived notice pages, each cited on its recall entity.
