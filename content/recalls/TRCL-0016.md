# Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) Portable Vaporizers (2026 Health Canada Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | Health Canada (consumer product recall; joint recall with the U.S. CPSC) |
| **Identification number** | RA-82210 |
| **Recall date** | June 18, 2026 |
| **Product** | Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) Portable Vaporizers (UPC 628078802274) |
| **Importer** | 7111495 Canada Inc. (dba Arizer Tech), Waterloo, Ontario |
| **Units** | 1,687 units sold in Canada; 5,000 sold in the United States |
| **Time period sold** | May 2025 through January 2026 |
| **Affected units** | Serial numbers beginning with `M3B1G5`, `M3F4G6`, `M35C43`, `M3PN54`, `M3SR42`, `M38G53`, `M3G576`, `M3C121` |
| **Hazard** | The internal lithium-ion battery can ignite or explode, posing fire and burn hazards |
| **Reported incidents** | No reports of incidents or injuries in Canada (as of June 2, 2026); four reports of incidents and no reports of injuries in the United States |
| **Remedy** | Immediately stop using the recalled product and contact 7111495 Canada Inc. for a free replacement |
| **Manufactured in** | China |

## Overview

Health Canada recalled the Arizer Solo III Intergalactic (Black) portable
vaporizer on June 18, 2026 because the internal lithium-ion battery can
ignite or explode, posing fire and burn hazards [^1]. Affected devices are
identified by UPC 628078802274 and eight serial-number prefixes etched on the
device bottom and printed on the packaging. 1,687 units were sold in Canada
from May 2025 through January 2026; the same recall covers approximately
5,000 units in the United States. The remedy is a free replacement unit from
the importer, 7111495 Canada Inc. (dba Arizer Tech) [^1].

Health Canada describes the notice as a joint recall with the U.S. Consumer
Product Safety Commission and the importer. This entity records the Canadian
side; the U.S. side is the CPSC recall 26-565 of the same date [^2], and both
are linked to the Solo III device record (TED-0001).
