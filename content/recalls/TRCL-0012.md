# Firewood 4 Vaporizers (2017 CPSC Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) |
| **Recall number** | 17-761 |
| **Recall date** | August 7, 2017 |
| **Product** | Firewood 4 vaporizers (all units) |
| **Distributor** | Firewood, of Wellesley, Mass. |
| **Units** | About 400 |
| **Affected units** | Units purchased between October 17, 2016 and June 27, 2017 that have not received the recall fix |
| **Hazard** | An electrical short can occur between the positive and negative terminals of the battery, which can cause the battery to catastrophically vent and catch fire, posing a fire hazard |
| **Reported incidents** | One report of an electrical short that allowed the battery to vent and catch fire; no injuries reported |
| **Remedy** | Repair — free return, rework, and return shipping from the manufacturer |
| **Sold at** | Firewoodvapes.com exclusively, October 2016 through June 2017, for $135–$185 |
| **Manufactured in** | United States |

## Overview

The CPSC announced a Fast Track recall of about 400 Firewood 4 vaporizers
because an electrical short between the battery terminals could cause the
lithium-ion battery to vent and catch fire [^1]. The firm received one report
of such a short; no injuries were reported [^1].

The manufacturer's recall notice identifies affected units by purchase window
(October 17, 2016 through June 27, 2017, fix not yet applied) rather than by
serial number [^2]. The repair is a manufacturing rework of the battery
compartment: each returned device is inspected for an adequate gap between the
battery positive-terminal wire lead and the top edge of the battery case;
devices with a gap that is too small are reworked to increase the gap to at
least 1 mm, and the gap is then filled with a non-conducting epoxy. Fixed
units are identifiable by the epoxy-filled gap near the positive battery
terminal [^2].

Consumers should stop using an affected device immediately and follow the
current CPSC and manufacturer instructions for the free repair. The original
consumer contact channel (recall@firewoodvapes.com) dates from 2017; the
recall predates the current firewoodaromatech.com storefront, whose FAQ still
carries the related battery guidance for Firewood 4/5/6/7 (flat-top 18650
cells rated ≥20 A continuous discharge) [^1][^2][^3].
