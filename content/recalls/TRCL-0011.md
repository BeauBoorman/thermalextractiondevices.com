# Arizer Solo III Portable Vaporizers (2026 CPSC Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) |
| **Recall number** | 26-565 |
| **Recall date** | June 18, 2026 |
| **Product** | Arizer Solo III portable vaporizers, model v2.0 |
| **Importer** | 7111495 Canada Inc. (dba Arizer) |
| **Affected color** | Intergalactic (Black) |
| **Units** | Approximately 5,000 |
| **Affected serial prefixes** | `M3B1G5`, `M3F4G6`, `M35C43`, `M3PN54`, `M3SR42`, `M38G53`, `M3G576`, `M3C121` |
| **Hazard** | The internal lithium-ion battery can explode or ignite, posing fire and burn hazards |
| **Reported incidents** | Four incidents of battery explosion or ignition |
| **Remedy** | Free Solo III V2 replacement unit, with disposal instructions from the importer and CPSC |

## Overview

The CPSC recalled approximately 5,000 Arizer Solo III portable vaporizers in
the Intergalactic (Black) color because the internal lithium-ion battery can
explode or ignite. The affected serial prefixes are printed on the packaging
and etched on the device bottom. The units were sold nationwide and on
arizer.com from May 2025 through January 2026 [^1].

Consumers should stop using an affected device immediately and follow the
current CPSC and Arizer instructions for replacement and disposal. Recalled
units should not be discarded in household trash or general recycling; use the
disposal process in the official notice [^1].
