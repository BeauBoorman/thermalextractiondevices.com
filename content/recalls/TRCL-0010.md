# Arizer Solo II Portable Vaporizers (2025 CPSC Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) |
| **Recall date** | September 18, 2025 |
| **Product** | Arizer Solo II portable vaporizers |
| **Importer** | 7111495 Canada Inc. (dba Arizer) |
| **Units** | Approximately 5,460 in the United States and 2,820 in Canada |
| **Affected units** | Serial numbers beginning with `M2` |
| **Hazard** | The internal lithium-ion battery can overheat, produce smoke, and/or eject material, posing fire and burn hazards |
| **Remedy** | Free Solo II MAX replacement unit, with disposal instructions from the importer and CPSC |

## Overview

The CPSC recalled affected Arizer Solo II portable vaporizers because of a
fire and burn hazard associated with the internal lithium-ion battery. The
recall identifies affected devices by serial numbers beginning with `M2` and
provides a free Solo II MAX replacement remedy [^1].

Consumers should stop using an affected device and follow the current CPSC and
Arizer instructions for replacement and disposal. Recalled units should not be
discarded in household trash or general recycling; use the disposal process in
the official notice [^1].

## Device Record Boundary

The archive currently models the Solo III as `devices/TED-0001`; it does not
yet have a separate Solo II device entity. This recall is therefore related to
the Arizer manufacturer record only and is not related to the Solo III record.
