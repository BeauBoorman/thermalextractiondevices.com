# Arizer Solo II Vaporizer (2025 Health Canada Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | Health Canada (consumer product recall) |
| **Identification number** | RA-77974 |
| **Recall date** | August 25, 2025 |
| **Product** | Arizer Solo II Vaporizer (UPC 628078800836) |
| **Importer** | 7111495 Canada Inc., Waterloo, Ontario |
| **Manufacturer** | Shenzhen En-Tron Technology Co. Ltd., Shenzhen, Guangdong, China |
| **Units** | 2,821 units of the affected products sold in Canada |
| **Time period sold** | June 2024 to July 2025 |
| **Affected units** | Serial numbers etched on the bottom of the device beginning with `M2` |
| **Hazard** | The battery can overheat, produce smoke, or eject material, posing a burn hazard; this can occur during charging, shortly after charging, or while the device is idle |
| **Reported incidents** | 2 reports of battery venting in Canada and no reports of injuries (as of August 20, 2025); 8 reports of battery venting with 1 emergency-room visit for smoke inhalation in the United States |
| **Remedy** | Immediately stop using the recalled product and contact Arizer for further instructions |
| **Manufactured in** | China |

## Overview

Health Canada recalled the Arizer Solo II Vaporizer on August 25, 2025
because the internal battery can overheat, produce smoke, or eject material,
posing a burn hazard — including during charging, shortly after charging, or
while idle [^1]. The notice identifies affected units by UPC 628078800836 and
serial numbers beginning with `M2` etched on the device bottom, and describes
the product as a portable micro-heater with a digital display, two control
buttons, a removable glass aroma tube, and a barrel-plug charging port, sold
in Mystic Blue and Carbon Black. 2,821 units were sold in Canada between June
2024 and July 2025. The Canadian importer is 7111495 Canada Inc.; the device
was manufactured by Shenzhen En-Tron Technology Co. Ltd. [^1]

This is the Canadian counterpart of the U.S. CPSC recall of September 18,
2025 [^2], which covers the same defect and serial-number scope
(≈5,460 US units and ≈2,820 Canadian units per the CPSC notice). The archive
models the Solo II as a prior Arizer model without a dedicated device entity;
the recall is linked to the manufacturer and to the CPSC counterpart entity.
