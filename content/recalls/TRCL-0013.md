# Firewood 4 Portable Vaporizers (2017 Health Canada Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | Health Canada (consumer product recall) |
| **Identification number** | RA-64880 |
| **Recall date** | October 20, 2017 |
| **Product** | Firewood 4 Portable Vaporizers |
| **Manufacturer** | Firewood Vaporizers Incorporated, Wellesley, Massachusetts, United States |
| **Units** | 15 units sold in Canada |
| **Time period sold** | November 4, 2016 to June 29, 2017, at Firewoodvapes.com |
| **Hazard** | An electrical short can occur in the vaporizer, posing a potential fire hazard and burn hazard |
| **Reported incidents** | No reports of incidents or injuries in Canada as of October 10, 2017 |
| **Remedy** | Stop using the product and contact Firewood Vaporizers Incorporated to arrange a free repair |

| **Archive status** | Notice archived by Health Canada (reference/recordkeeping copy) |

## Overview

Health Canada recalled the Firewood 4 Portable Vaporizer on October 20, 2017
because an electrical short inside the device could pose fire and burn
hazards [^1]. The notice describes a wood-bodied portable measuring 5 cm wide
by 9 cm tall by 2 cm thick with a sliding battery compartment cover, sold
finished or unfinished, with the Firewood logo printed on the bottom. Fifteen
units were sold in Canada through Firewoodvapes.com between November 4, 2016
and June 29, 2017. The remedy was a free repair arranged directly with the
manufacturer [^1].

This is the Canadian counterpart of the U.S. CPSC recall 17-761 issued for
the same defect on August 7, 2017 [^2]. Both notices describe the same
hazard; the CPSC notice additionally documents the terminal-gap rework that
constitutes the repair.
