# HEXO All-in-One Disposable Cannabis Vape Pens (2021 Health Canada Recall)

## Recall Notice

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Recall authority** | Health Canada (consumer product recall; subcategory cannabis) |
| **Identification number** | RA-74809 |
| **Recall date** | January 22, 2021 |
| **Products** | Three types of HEXO-branded all-in-one disposable vape pens prefilled with cannabis: Durban, Blue Dream, and Trainwreck |
| **Distributor** | HEXO Operations Inc. (DBA HEXO), Belleville, Ontario |
| **Device manufacturer** | Green Tank Technologies, Toronto, Ontario |
| **Units** | Approximately 100,132 units sold in Canada |
| **Time period sold** | May 2020 to January 2021 |
| **Hazard** | The auto turn-off mechanism can fail, resulting in continuous battery engagement, excessive heat, and melting of the plastic shell |
| **Reported incidents** | Three reports of incidents; no reports of injuries in Canada or elsewhere as of January 18, 2021 |
| **Remedy** | Stop using the recalled products and return them to the point of sale for a full refund; registered medical clients were contacted by HEXO directly |
| **Device origin** | Manufactured in China |
| **Affected lots** | Durban: AAA-112830-A/-B/-C, AAA-112703, AAA-112723; Blue Dream: AAA-112821-A/-B, AAA-112705, AAA-112725; Trainwreck: AAA-112787-A/-B/-C/-D, AAA-112704, AAA-112724 |

## Overview

Health Canada recalled three types of HEXO-branded all-in-one disposable
cannabis vape pens on January 22, 2021 because a mechanical defect could
cause the auto turn-off mechanism to fail, leaving the battery continuously
engaged, generating excessive heat, and melting the device's plastic shell
[^1]. Approximately 100,132 units were sold in Canada from May 2020 to
January 2021 across named lots of the Durban, Blue Dream, and Trainwreck
prefilled pens. The device was manufactured by Green Tank Technologies and
distributed by HEXO Operations Inc. Consumers were instructed to return the
pens to the point of sale for a full refund [^1].

This event is a cannabis-product recall with a device-level hardware cause
(battery control failure) recorded by the archive for thermal-extraction
safety coverage. No HEXO hardware entity is modeled in the devices catalog,
and no HEXO organization or product entity exists in the archive; the recall
is therefore linked to the Canada jurisdiction only.
